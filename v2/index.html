The problem

The tools do more, but your team is still the glue.

What people do between AIs today

  • Copy context into each private chat
  • Re-explain the process to every tool
  • Paste outputs into tickets, CRM and chat
  • Re-run one-off automations by hand
  • Ask around: what ran, what changed?
  • Chase decisions across threads

AI made individuals more capable. Team work around it is still scattered across tools, private chats and one-off automations.

Individual power, on its own, doesn’t create shared ownership of a process, reuse of what one person built, or visibility into what needs a decision.

Problem framing from founder-approved positioning; to be validated with buyers02 / 18
The metaphor

Today’s agents are hired like freelancers. Companies run on teams.

Freelancer mode

  • Briefed from scratch each time
  • Context lives in one person’s account
  • Output handed back for a person to carry on
  • Nobody else sees what’s running

Company mode — an AI team

  • Shared company context everyone works from
  • Each process has a role and a named owner
  • Handoffs between bots and people are visible
  • One decision queue for what needs a human

A metaphor for disconnected usage versus coordinated work — not a claim that other products lack team features. Claude Code, Hermes and similar tools are capable; we aim to complement them by making the team, not the individual session, the unit of work.

Ownership & model choice

Your company owns the intelligence it builds. Models are chosen per job.

Company-owned

Knowledge · processes · ways of working · decisions & history · bots

Shared workspace

Owners · permissions · approvals · run history

Supported models

Chosen per bot; a switch goes live only after replay on recent runs and owner approval

We mean
  • Company knowledge and processes, not tied to one model or personal account
  • Context stays put when a model changes
We don’t mean
  • Ownership of people or third-party model weights
  • Universal compatibility, pooled subscriptions, or a finished export-and-restore — to be built and demonstrated first

Market context

Team AI is already a buying category — the open question is who owns cross-tool processes.

Suites bundle it

Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Workspace Studio, ChatGPT Business, Claude Team sell team AI to their installed base.

Specialists sell agents to functions

Dust, Juma, Relevance AI, Glean package shared agents and context.

Teams build their own

n8n, Dify, LibreChat, Open WebUI let technical teams assemble workflows and self-host.

No market size claimed. We have no qualified count for our initial segment yet; M3 work will build one bottom-up (see slide 10) rather than sum AI categories.

Differentiation hypothesis

Hypothesis: we win when one function’s processes are owned, reviewed and portable.

What we’d need to be better at

  • Processes as owned, versioned assets with change review by the owner
  • One decision queue across a function’s bots
  • Model switches gated by replay on real runs
  • Export of knowledge + processes with proven restore

Head-to-head proof (by M6)

Same escalation workflow, same design partner, built in Silow, Dust and n8n. Measure:

  • Time to first approved run
  • Distinct people running it weekly at week 4
  • Approval wait time
  • Owner-rated quality on 50 replayed tickets

If we don’t lead on ≥2, we narrow or change the bet.

Hypothesis, not a finding · uniqueness and willingness to pay untested12 / 18
