Silow · Team AIThe problem

The tools do more, but your team is still the glue.

AI made individuals more capable. The work between AIs is still carried by hand.

Individual power alone doesn’t create shared ownership of a process, reuse of what one person built, or visibility into what needs a decision.

Problem framing from founder-approved positioning · to be validated with buyers02
Silow · Team AIThe metaphor

Today’s agents are hired like freelancers. Companies run on teams.

Freelancer mode
  • Briefed from scratch each time
  • Context lives in one person’s account
  • Output handed back for a person to carry on
  • Nobody else sees what’s running
Company mode — an AI team
  • Shared company context everyone works from
  • Each process has a role and a named owner
  • Handoffs between bots and people are visible
  • One decision queue for what needs a human
A metaphor, not a claim that other products lack team features · approved by Egor and Nikita03

Silow · Team AIOwnership & model choice

Your company owns the intelligence it builds. Models are chosen per job.

Product vision
We mean
  • Company knowledge and processes, not tied to one model or personal account
  • Context stays put when a model changes
We don’t mean
  • Ownership of people or third-party model weights
  • Universal compatibility, pooled subscriptions, or finished export-and-restore — to be built and demonstrated first
Portability is a proof milestone, not a shipped feature08
Silow · Team AIMarket context

Team AI is already a buying category. The open question: who owns cross-tool processes?

Suites bundle it

Microsoft 365 Copilot, Google Workspace Studio, ChatGPT Business, Claude Team — team AI sold to the installed base.

Specialists sell to functions

Dust, Juma, Relevance AI, Glean — shared agents and context, packaged.

Teams build their own

n8n, Dify, LibreChat, Open WebUI — technical teams assemble workflows and self-host.

No market size claimed. We have no qualified count for our initial segment yet — M3 builds one bottom-up, not by summing AI categories.

Silow · Team AIDifferentiation
Hypothesis, not a finding

We win when one function’s processes are owned, reviewed and portable.

  • Processes as owned, versioned assets with owner change review
  • One decision queue across a function’s bots
  • Model switches gated by replay on real runs
  • Export of knowledge + processes with proven restore
